# Code of Conduct

We want Ultimate Quickshell to be a welcoming technical project.

Participants are expected to:
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on code, design, or technical decisions rather than people.
- Assume good intent while still addressing harmful behavior.
- Welcome contributors with different levels of experience.
- Avoid harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and deliberate disruption.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the project constructive.
